This page is dedicated to explaining what all the other pages do and how to operate them.

Default Views

Employees

The "Employees" page serves as a comprehensive hub for managing personnel within your organization. Here, administrators have the ability to not only create, edit, and delete employee profiles but also schedule employees directly from this interface. The page offers detailed access to each employee's profile, where you can view their current schedule, process requests for time off, and maintain detailed notes, enhancing individual employee oversight. Additionally, this page features a "Not Active" tab, ideal for managing seasonal or temporarily inactive employees, allowing for easy toggling of their active status as needed.

Recommended Device:

Schedule

The "Schedule" page is designed to optimize the management and organization of employee work shifts. This intuitive interface allows for the arrangement of shifts in chronological order, from the earliest to the latest, and displays the total number of personnel assigned to each day. It identifies employees with multiple shifts in a single day or those recommended for time off. For enhanced communication, schedules can be disseminated directly via text, email, or printed out. A particularly useful feature is the ability to copy two weeks of schedules forward, provided the subsequent two weeks are clear; this facilitates consistent scheduling and eases future planning tasks.

Recommended Device:

Punch Clock

The "Punch Clock" page is specifically designed to facilitate the time tracking needs of your workforce. Displaying a comprehensive list of employees, this page allows each individual to clock in at the start of their shifts. Upon clocking in, the system begins tracking their work hours immediately. If the employee is designated as a floor employee, their status in the 'Tables' view is automatically updated to 'active,' providing real-time visibility into staff availability. Employees can clock out by selecting their profile, which not only ends their shift but also logs the details for managerial review and record-keeping purposes. This seamless integration ensures accurate time management and operational efficiency.

Recommended Device:

Tables

The "Tables View" page offers an interactive representation of your establishment's floor layout. Users can configure the display scale to suit their needs, providing a flexible view of all floor plans. This module is crucial for managing where clocked-in floor employees are assigned, showing which tables they are overseeing and the occupancy status of each table, including whether it is actively hosting guests. Additionally, this view integrates a real-time waitlist showing all active entries for the day. The system allows for direct communication with guests via text to notify them when their table is ready. Users can customize table shapes, seating arrangements, and the overall floor plan. Mass assignment of tables to employees is streamlined through the interface, with capabilities to view specific assignments per employee and filter tables based on activity status or seating capacity. The view is highly modular, enabling users to switch between different floor layouts and toggle the visibility of the table scroller. Enhanced interactivity is provided through drag-and-drop functionality for table positioning and double-tap actions to toggle their active status.

Recommended Device:

Reservations

The "Reservations" page enables efficient management of guest reservations across any specified day. When a reservation is set, it automatically populates the day's waitlist, allowing for streamlined organization and visibility. This feature allows users to set a maximum number of reservations for each time slot, a parameter that can be adjusted in the managerial settings to accommodate varying business needs. The interface provides robust functionalities to edit, delete, and monitor all upcoming reservations, ensuring optimal management of guest flow. This platform is designed to be user-friendly and is highly recommended for use on both phones and tablets, facilitating on-the-go management without the need for a stationary setup.

Managerial Views

Profile Editor

The "Profile Editor" page offers comprehensive tools for establishment owners to enhance their public-facing profiles. This interface allows for the customization of profile photos displayed on the customer-facing website, ensuring a personalized touch. Additionally, owners can input essential contact information for the restaurant, including details about the owner, location name, and the website URL. A unique feature is the ability to set the maximum number of overlapping reservations, which aids in managing guest capacity efficiently. Users can also configure default alert texts for tables, enhancing communication with staff during service. The page supports rich content creation, allowing the addition of a detailed establishment description with the option to include basic HTML and CSS for more advanced customization. Furthermore, the 'Floor Assigner' section facilitates the creation and naming of different floors, which seamlessly integrate with the Table Manager to reflect accurate layouts in real-time. This page is ideally used on both phones for on-the-go management and computers for more detailed content creation.

Recommended Device:

Hiring Dashboard

The "Hiring Dashboard" page empowers employers to create and promote job openings directly from their dashboard. Each job posting can be customized with an optional job icon, enhancing the visual appeal and recognition of the position. Employers can define the job type, provide a detailed job description, specify the job name, and indicate the total number of available positions. This comprehensive setup allows potential applicants to gain a clear understanding of what each position entails. The interface includes a filtering option by job name, enabling employers to manage listings more efficiently. All job details are updated and reflected in real-time on the Servingu website, where prospective employees can sign in with Google and submit their resumes directly. This seamless integration ensures a streamlined hiring process and attracts a wide range of qualified candidates.

Recommended Device:

Hours Editor

The "Hours Editor" page is a crucial tool for managing employee work hours recorded via the Punch Clock screen. This interface allows managers to select specific time ranges to review the documented hours of employees. In instances where an employee may have forgotten to clock in or out, this page provides the functionality to manually add, edit, or delete hours, ensuring accurate and up-to-date record-keeping. For extended time ranges, the system offers an export feature, which compiles and displays the hours in an Excel sheet format. This capability is especially useful for payroll preparation and auditing, streamlining the administrative workload associated with managing employee hours.

Recommended Device:

Resume Reviewer

The "Resume Reviewer" page is designed to streamline the recruitment process by facilitating the review of resumes submitted through the ServingU website. This interface captures essential applicant information including their name, email, the specific job they applied for, and their resume. Users have the convenience of downloading resumes directly for detailed review and possess the applicant's email for easy outreach. Additionally, each application features a timestamp, providing visibility into when it was submitted. For organizational purposes, resumes can be archived for later review or deleted if they do not meet the job criteria. This flexible and efficient system is optimized for use across all devices, ensuring accessibility whether on the go or at work.

Recommended Device:

Menu Builder

The "Menu Builder" page offers a robust platform for restaurants to design and manage their menus directly. Users can start from scratch to craft their restaurant's menu, creating sections each with its own description. Within these sections, menu items can be added, complete with descriptions, pricing, and tags to characterize the dish, enhancing menu navigation and customer understanding. Each menu item can also include an optional photo, adding a visual appeal. Users have the flexibility to manage the order of both sections and individual menu items to ensure the menu flows logically and is aligned with dining experiences. Additionally, an overarching description can be added to the entire menu, providing a comprehensive overview of what customers can expect. All this information is seamlessly integrated into the ServingU website, where it can be accessed by customers through a QR code, making it easy for them to view the menu on their own devices.
